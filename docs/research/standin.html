Evaluation: the local stand-in at a session limit

Issue: #168 · Date: 2026-10-14 · Status: complete

Question​

On 2026-10-13 the owner's 5-hour limit ran out at 21:49, with the main rule's ifDown pointing at qwen/qwen3.8-27b. The session did not keep working on the local model, as ifDown promises. Why not, and what does it take?

Decision​

  • Fix what stopped the session:
    • a tool search's tool_reference blocks reach the local model as text (#169)
    • Janus loads an unloaded model itself, at contextTokens or 65,536 tokens (#170)
    • a stand-in on a conversation too long for the local model leaves out older turns (#171)
    • a stand-in waits for a busy model instead of failing (#172)
  • Auto mode's safety checks stay with Claude by default, and the console says to leave auto mode when one cannot reach Claude (#173). A safety rule with ifDown is documented as an opt-in. The local model can answer the checks in the expected format, but they carry the conversation's transcript: a check late in a long session is too large for any local context, and a short one took 46 s.
  • The local model's answers were not the problem. In the four turns it carried, it did what Claude would have done: it ran the right commands, and when they were refused it said why and worked around it.

What happened​

From the ledger and captures of 2026-10-13, 21:49 to 21:59:

TimeRequestWhat happened
21:49:59Main session, 604 messages, ~417k tokens429; no stand-in, because the request fits no local context
21:50:53Fresh session after /clear, ~33.7k tokens429; no stand-in, because the model was not loaded and a cold start was capped at 32,768 tokens (#149)
21:52–21:57Same session, after the model was loaded by hand at 107,008 tokensFour local answers, 21–84 s each. Every Bash call was refused: the safety checks went to Claude and got 429s, which Claude Code reports as "temporarily unavailable"
21:59:00Same session, after a ToolSearchLM Studio 400: request.messages.8.content.0.content.0.type: Invalid literal value, expected "text", the tool_reference block. Every later turn would fail the same way

A fresh Claude Code main turn is 25–35k tokens before any work: the system prompt, CLAUDE.md, and the tool definitions.

Checks after the fixes​

  • The 21:59 request, replayed on qwen3.8-27b with the tool_reference fix: a tool call in 21.5 s.
  • The 21:49 request (1.26 MB, ~417k tokens), sent through a scratch Janus whose upstream always answers 429, with older turns left out: a sensible tool call (git branch, git status) in 87 s. LM Studio counted 52,910 input tokens. Janus estimates bytes / 3, which came to about 80k, so the estimate errs on the safe side.
  • Safety checks. The 21:47:59 check was ~157k tokens, the whole transcript, and could not be replayed. The 21:53:26 check (~32k tokens), replayed: <severity>5 in 7 output tokens, stopped at </severity>, in 46 s. The scale is 0–100 with 50 the allow/block line, and the action was a read-only git log/tag/status, so 5 is sound. Thinking is disabled on these requests, so the 64-token budget is enough.

What this does not show​

  • The quality of a long stand-in session. Four turns were seen on the day, and one turn each in the checks above.
  • Whether leaving out the middle of a conversation leads the model to repeat work or contradict decisions made there. The note it is given says the middle is missing; how well it copes was not measured.
  • The local model's verdicts on harmful actions. One safety check, of a harmless command, was replayed.
  • Memory. The model ran on LM Studio at studio.local at a 107k context; what 65,536 tokens costs on the owner's 36 GB Mac was not measured.

When to stand in (#150)​

Added 2026-10-14. Until then, any 429 or 5xx made the local model stand in. Claude Code retries those itself, so a two-second overload could put a local model's turn into a conversation for good.

What the headers say. The ledger keeps retry-after, x-should-retry and the anthropic-ratelimit-* headers on every failed upstream response. Over the 74 failures recorded to 2026-10-14:

  • Every session-limit 429 had anthropic-ratelimit-unified-status: rejected and a retry-after equal to the seconds left until the 5-hour limit reset: 9,720 (2.7 hours) on 2026-10-13, 5,745 on 2026-10-14. anthropic-ratelimit-unified-reset gave the same time.
  • x-should-retry was true on all of them, hours from the reset, so it does not tell a long limit from a short one.
  • Claude Code's start-up quota check got a 429 with no rate-limit headers. It is its own class, and no rule stands in for it.
  • No 529 ("overloaded") or 5xx was seen.

Decision. Stand in at once when a 429's retry-after, or its unified reset when rejected, is a minute or more away. Otherwise relay the failure to Claude Code to retry, and stand in only when Claude has failed three times within two minutes with no answer between: an outage, not a blip. An answer from Claude clears the count.

What this does not show. How a 529 or an unreachable Anthropic looks in practice, since none happened; the three-in-two-minutes threshold rests on Claude Code's backoff, not a measured outage. Whether a short 429 (seconds from its reset) ever happens on a subscription.
